# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 07 July 2026

## Purpose

This document explains how data is processed when using [**OctoPrint-PrettyGCode**](https://github.com/jacopotediosi/OctoPrint-PrettyGCode), a plugin for [OctoPrint](https://github.com/OctoPrint/OctoPrint), an open-source 3D printer management software.

This policy represents a **best-effort attempt to transparently inform users** and **does not constitute legal advice**.

This policy applies specifically to the OctoPrint-PrettyGCode plugin and not to OctoPrint itself or other plugins.

## Data Collection and Use

### OctoPrint Tracking

OctoPrint itself (not OctoPrint-PrettyGCode) may collect **anonymous usage data** through its **opt-in tracking**. This tracking occurs only if the user has explicitly enabled it.

- For more details, see [**OctoPrint Tracking**](https://tracking.octoprint.org/).

### Data Handling by the Plugin Maintainers

The plugin does **not** collect, store, or share **any** data with its maintainers. Everything the plugin does happens locally, inside the user's own web browser: no information is ever sent elsewhere, and nothing leaves the user's device.

### Integration with Other Plugins

OctoPrint-PrettyGCode may integrate with additional OctoPrint plugins, e.g. [OctoPrint-Dashboard](https://plugins.octoprint.org/plugins/dashboard/).

Installation of these plugins is **strictly manual** and must be performed through the installation mechanisms offered by OctoPrint.

Users must consult the respective plugin's privacy policy prior to use.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated over time. The current version is always available at [PRIVACY.md](https://github.com/jacopotediosi/OctoPrint-PrettyGCode/blob/master/PRIVACY.md).
